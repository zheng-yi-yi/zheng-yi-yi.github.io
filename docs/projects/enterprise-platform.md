# Enterprise Platform

As a Full-Stack Engineer at a global manufacturing enterprise, I am part of the core team building a **unified internal application platform** and the **manufacturing operations application** built on top of it. Together they move the business from fragmented standalone tools to a connected digital ecosystem.

## The Platform
A multi-tenant PaaS platform for internal applications, dashboards and shared services. It acts as a single entry point with shared identity, enabling standardization, faster delivery and scalable growth through reusable services across digital teams.

### Strategic Goals
- **Consistent user experience**: Standardizing UI/UX across internal tools.
- **Less duplication**: Reducing integration effort through shared architectural patterns.
- **Better visibility**: Breaking down data silos via a shared data tier.
- **Faster scaling**: Reusing common services to roll out quickly to sites worldwide.

## Manufacturing Operations Application
A custom-built application for manufacturing operations, built on the platform to deliver a consistent digital experience.

### Why Build In-House
Off-the-shelf tools could not fully meet the specific needs of our manufacturing sites. Building in-house let us incorporate direct feedback from frontline users and deliver the features that were previously missing.

### Core Capabilities
- **Tiered Manufacturing Support**: Toolkits for every operational level, from site down to individual work centers.
- **Interactive Visual Platform**: Near real-time performance metrics for fast, data-driven decision making.
- **Deep Integration**: Connects with enterprise systems such as MES, data platforms (e.g. Snowflake), BI tools and Grafana.
- **Enterprise SaaS Features**: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), multi-site tenant design and zero-downtime upgrades.

## Impact
These systems address inconsistent UI/UX, fragmented system access and high long-term maintenance costs, helping users make data-driven decisions that improve efficiency and productivity across a global manufacturing footprint.
